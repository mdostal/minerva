Brand Voice

Firefly Events uses a warm, approachable, and celebratory tone. The brand embodies joy, connection, and unforgettable moments. When writing for Firefly, emphasize community, celebration, and attention to detail.

Visual Identity

Primary colors: Deep Purple (#6B46C1), Warm Gold (#F59E0B), Soft Cream (#FEF3C7). Typography: Use Montserrat for headings, Inter for body text.

Tone Guidelines

Do: Be warm, inclusive, and detail-oriented. Celebrate milestones. Use vivid, sensory language.
Don't: Be overly formal, corporate, or transactional. Avoid jargon.

How to Apply

When Minerva generates content for Firefly Events (emails, web copy, social posts), this memory should be loaded to ensure brand consistency. Related: [[firefly-event-templates]], [[firefly-client-preferences]]
