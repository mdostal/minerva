# Q — Audience (who operates/consumes Minerva)
**BOTH — human-initiated AND service-initiated.** A human operator (me) feeds ideas in directly,
AND Auriga / other Pantheon services invoke it programmatically. Minerva needs a **human entry
point** *and* a **service (subprocess-ABI) interface** — the same idea can arrive from a person or
from Auriga. Not "just me" (drops the Auriga path) and not "services only" (drops the human feed).
